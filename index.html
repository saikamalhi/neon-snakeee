⌖ HOW TO PLAY

Guide the snake around the arena and collect glowing energy cells. Each cell adds 10 points and makes your snake longer. The game gets faster as your score climbs.

⚡ LEVEL UP

Every 50 points advances your level. Higher levels increase the challenge, so plan your turns and watch the space ahead.

⌨ QUICK CONTROLS

Use Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D to move. Press Space to pause or resume. Touch players can use the on-screen direction pad.
